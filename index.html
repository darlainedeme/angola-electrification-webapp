About this plan

Three side-by-side scenarios: 53% (Compact target, do-minimum), 60% (minimum credible floor given the Secretary of State's signal), and 70% (upside under a staged delivery approach). Baseline calibrated to 51% access in 2025 (WB-reconciled value; Compact's literal 45% is challenged in the critique).

Densification = pop growth in already-electrified baseline clusters ($1,437/conn blended ENDE LOTES rate). Extension = newly served grid clusters ($1,437/conn). SHS = off-grid clusters that would have been mini-grid in OnSSET, reclassified to SHS per the no-MG rule ($282/conn). Source: OnSSET/GEP V.3 Scenario C, file ao-3-1_1_0_0_1_1.csv.

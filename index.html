Compact comparison

The MINEA Compact claims $4.6B for 1.7M connections to reach 53% access. This analysis finds reaching 70% requires across 2.31M connections. The gap reflects a unit-cost reconciliation: Compact's implied $2,700 per connection vs the $1,437 blended ENDE LOTES rate (after 11% standard add-ons), plus modality transparency.
